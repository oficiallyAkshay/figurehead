Most of the new glyphs added for the chart style are simplified from Lucide (https://lucide.dev), ISC licence, copyright (c) Lucide Contributors. Full licence text: https://lucide.dev/license. The exception is "git-merge", traced from examples/pierless/reference.svg's own hand-inked button icon rather than from Lucide.
